Cricket

The grid already has the right structure. It needs to stretch and its numbers need to be readable from arm's length.

  • The seven rows share all the height between the score cards and the undo row. Nothing sits in an empty band at the bottom.
  • Numbers grow from about 20px to 34px. Marks stay as dots under each number and keep their own column on the left.
  • Shading stays on alternate rows so a row is easy to track with a thumb.
  • Undo dart and Undo turn sit on the bottom edge of the screen, not floating below the grid.

Open question: the cricket grid can also drop the input boxes on this screen, because the grid already shows the turn. Say if you want that.

121 checkout

The target and remaining score are what people look at between darts. They get the large type, and the pad fills the rest of the screen.

  • Remaining grows to about 60px. Target stays smaller beside it, so the two numbers are not the same weight.
  • Attempt, darts left and best go on one line under the tiles, which frees a row.
  • The pad takes the leftover height, with digits at 44px like the other games.
  • Undo score and End run sit on the bottom edge, so the empty band below the buttons goes away.

Sizes are shown on a 300px wide mockup. On a real phone the digits scale with the screen, so the proportions hold.
